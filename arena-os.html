ARENA OS:
Prize Payout Approval

A Make.com workflow that turns a manual prize-payout decision into a structured approval system: capture payout inputs, route the decision, record the outcome, notify stakeholders, and return a clean final status.

Make.comWorkflow AutomationGoogle SheetsEmail AutomationDecision Logic

The problem

Prize payouts involve multiple hand-offs: the payout details need to be captured accurately, an approver must make a decision, the decision needs an audit trail, and the relevant recipient needs to be informed. Manual coordination creates delays and inconsistent records.

The system

The workflow accepts a payout ID, tournament ID, winner, amount, decision, approver email and recipient email. It then branches into an approve/reject flow and completes the corresponding record-and-notification actions.

01 · InputsPayout ID, tournament, winner, amount and decision.
02 · DecisionApprove / reject routing with conditional logic.
03 · RecordAppend a structured outcome to the payout sheet.
04 · NotifySend the appropriate approval or hold email.
05 · ReturnReturn final status, payout ID and result message.

Two controlled outcomes

Approved

Writes an approved payout record, triggers the approval communication and returns an APPROVED status.

Rejected / Hold

Writes a rejected payout record, sends a review/hold communication and returns a REJECTED status.

What it demonstrates

  • Process mapping and workflow design
  • Conditional business logic
  • Automated record keeping
  • Cross-tool integration
  • Operational controls and auditability

Why it matters

The project translates an operational process into a repeatable system. Instead of treating automation as a collection of disconnected actions, the flow is designed around a business decision and its downstream consequences.
